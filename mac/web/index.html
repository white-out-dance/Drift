1
Plug in your iPhone

Unlock it and tap Trust if asked.

2
Turn on Developer Mode

Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. The phone restarts once.

3
Install Drift on the iPhone

Signs Drift with the free Apple ID in your Xcode and installs it. Re-run weekly (free Apple IDs sign for 7 days).


      
4
Trust it on the iPhone
        
Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › your Apple ID › Trust.

      
5
Get LocalDevVPN
        
Free on the App Store. A loopback connection that lets Drift reach the phone's own developer services.

        Open App Store page

      
6
Pairing file
        
Creates a pairing just for Drift and copies it into the app. A copy is saved to your Desktop.

On the iPhone: turn on LocalDevVPN, open Drift, pick a place, tap Spoof.
